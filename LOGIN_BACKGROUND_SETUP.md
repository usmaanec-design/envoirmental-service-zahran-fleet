# Beautiful Login & Welcome Screen Background Setup

## Image Setup Instructions

To complete the beautiful login and welcome screen setup, you need to replace the placeholder with your actual building image:

### Steps:

1. **Save your building image** as `building-background.jpg` in the `public/images/` directory
2. **Image requirements**:
   - Format: JPG, PNG, or WebP
   - Recommended resolution: 1920x1080 or higher
   - File size: Under 2MB for optimal loading
